How the board works

What you submit

A completed Monday–Sunday week in Asia/Kolkata (IST), INR-denominated starting account equity and net weekly PnL after brokerage, taxes and fees. Use the change in total account equity across the period, including changes in unrealised positions. Do not mix realised-only results with total-equity results.

Fair comparisons

This pilot only accepts weeks with no deposits or withdrawals. Return = net weekly PnL ÷ starting equity × 100. Entries are ranked by return; exact equal returns share a rank. This does not measure risk, leverage, drawdown or long-term skill. A weekly leader is not a recommendation.

Review and corrections

One entry per account per week. Results remain private until approved. Admins check submitted evidence; screenshots may be incomplete or altered. “Reviewed” does not mean broker-verified. Admins may revoke approval and retain an audit trail. Contact the operator for corrections; do not create additional accounts to evade the limit.

Privacy

We store your email, display name, password hash, session records, submitted figures, reflection, evidence and review history. Approved display names, figures and reflections are public. Emails and evidence are visible only where needed for account operation and review. Evidence is accessible only to its owner and admins.

Redact personal identifiers before uploading. Do not upload another person's account information. This pilot retains records until the operator processes a deletion request. Contact the operator privately to request correction or deletion; backup retention must be disclosed before public launch. No analytics or advertising cookies are used; an essential session cookie keeps you signed in.

Limitations

Email ownership is not verified. Account recovery is operator-assisted. No broker connections, trade execution, signals or investment advice are provided. Past performance does not predict future results.
