THE IDEA BEHIND TRADE BULL

A place for honest trading reflection.

Trade Bull is a community project for sharing weekly performance and learning from each other's process. A losing week can teach as much as a profitable one.

Transparency takes work.

We collect structured results, keep supporting evidence private, and review entries before publication. We openly describe the limits of that review.
